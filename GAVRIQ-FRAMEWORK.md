# Gavriq Open Framework — Handshake

This repository has completed its handshake with the **Gavriq Open Framework**.

## Who we are

The Gavriq Open Framework is the **mentoring and consulting framework** for developing
applications under this account. It is a body of methodology, guardrails, templates, agent
definitions and earned do's/don'ts, distilled from real build cycles.

## What that means for this repository

- **Consult first.** Before designing or building a new feature, module or pipeline here,
  consult the framework: its SDLC pipeline (requirements through production), its
  do's/don'ts guardrail checklist, its governance (check-in / gate) pattern, and its
  agent-memory schema.
- **Mentor, not owner.** The framework advises. This repository's own owners, `AGENTS.md`,
  `CLAUDE.md`, and security/compliance rules remain authoritative where they differ.
- **Learnings flow back.** Incident-driven rules, gate designs and retrospectives that
  emerge here are worth feeding back to the framework so every other application benefits.

## For humans and agents starting work here

1. Read this file, then the repo's own `README.md` and any `AGENTS.md` / `CLAUDE.md`.
2. Apply the framework's guardrails and pipeline stages to the work you are about to do.
3. Record anything the framework got wrong or missed, so it can be improved.
